Gregg's Deviled Eggs

Ingredients:

  • 1 medium-large sized saucepan
  • 1 medium sized mixing bowl
  • 12 large white eggs
  • 5 1/4 tablespoons of mayonnaise
  • 4 teaspoons of mustard

Directions:

  1. Grab your saucepan and fill it up 75% of the way with tap water.
  2. Place the pan on a burner and then carefully drop your eggs in.
  3. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and set a timer for 12 minutes.
  4. The next step could be done in multiple different ways:
    • Once the timer ends, pour the water out and keep the eggs in. Then fill the saucepan with ice water.
    • Before the timer ends, grab another bowl and fill with ice water. Then transfer the eggs from the saucepan.
  5. Once the ice has melted, start peeling the eggshells.
  6. After all the eggs have been peeled, grab a small knife and cut each egg in half, lengthwise.
  7. After all 12 eggs are cut in half, grab a spoon and begin collecting the yolk to put into the mixing bowl.
  8. Take your spoon and smoosh all of the yolk in the mixing bowl.
  9. After crushing the yolk, pour stir in the rest of the ingredients
    • 5 1/4 tablespoons of mayonnaise
    • 4 teaspoons of mustard
    • A dash of salt and pepper
  10. Mix!
  11. Once the mixture has reached a nice, smooth-ish consistency, take your spoon and start scooping the yolk back into each eggshell.
  12. After each eggshell is full again, add a sprinkle of paprika on each one.
  13. Serve and enjoy!
